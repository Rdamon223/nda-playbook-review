## Fallbacks (playbook wording to propose)

**7. Compelled disclosure** (section 6). The clause has the 'only the portion required' limit but lacks the notice and cooperation obligations. No Escalate trigger applies because the limit on what may be disclosed exists, so the written fallback fixes it.
> If the Receiving Party is required by law or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, it may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to d...

Propose: "If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, reasonably cooperate at the Disclosing Party's expense with any effort to obtain a protective order, and disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose."

- **6. Permitted disclosure to representatives** (section 5): Covers the full range of Representatives on a need-to-know basis with equivalent confidentiality obligations, and the Receiving Party is responsible for their breaches.

- **14. No license, no warranty, no obligation to proceed** (section 9): All three statements are present and mutual, and there is no feedback license, exclusivity or standstill.
