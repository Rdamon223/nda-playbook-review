## Escalations (a lawyer decides)

**1. Mutuality** (section 2). Section 2 is mutual, but the remedies clause (Section 11) protects only Larkspur's information, so the NDA is one-way in effect for remedies. The playbook says to escalate where a separate clause makes it one-way.
> the obligations in this Agreement apply equally to both parties.

**11. Remedies and injunctive relief** (section 11). The equitable relief clause runs only in Larkspur's favor, so the Company's Confidential Information gets no equivalent acknowledgment. The playbook fallback covers a one-direction clause, but a lawyer should decide given the effect on mutuality (topic 1). The fees clause is neutral.
> The Receiving Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Larkspur’s Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and Larkspur may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy.

## Fallbacks (playbook wording to propose)

**4. Term of the agreement and survival of obligations** (section 7). The two-year term and early termination right are fine. Survival of 18 months is under two years but not under one year, so the fallback applies. The NDA has no trade secret carve-out, which the fallback adds.
> The Receiving Party’s obligations under this Agreement survive for eighteen (18) months after expiration or termination.

Propose: "The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement survive for three (3) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, except that obligations with respect to trade secrets survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law."
