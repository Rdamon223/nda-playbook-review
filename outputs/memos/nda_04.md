## Escalations (a lawyer decides)

**10. Non-solicitation or non-compete language** (section 9). This is a non-solicit covering customers and suppliers, and it also bars accepting business, which is a restriction on doing business. It runs 24 months after the term. Both are escalate triggers.
> neither party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit or accept business from any customer or supplier of the other party identified to it through the other party’s Confidential Information.

**11. Remedies and injunctive relief** (section 11). Only the Counterparty (Pellucid) may recover legal fees, and the Company (Oakhaven) must pay them regardless of outcome. This is an escalate trigger.
> In any action to enforce this Agreement, Pellucid is entitled to recover from Oakhaven its reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs.

## Fallbacks (playbook wording to propose)

**13. Assignment and change of control** (section 12). The clause bars all assignment, including in a merger or change of control, without consent. It applies equally to both parties and has no escalate trigger, so the written fallback applies.
> Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement, whether voluntarily, by operation of law, by merger, or by change of control, without the other party’s prior written consent.

Propose: "Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, on written notice to the other party."
